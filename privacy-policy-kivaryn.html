Privacy Policy — Kivaryn

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Kivaryn, developed and published by Marks Dec.

It explains what information may be collected or processed when you use the game, how that information may be used, and what choices may be available to you.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, home address, identification documents, precise or approximate location, contacts, call history, text messages, photographs, videos, personal files, financial information, passwords, or private communications.

Kivaryn does not require you to create a personal user account or provide personal profile information in order to play.

2. Technical Information That May Be Collected

The game and services used to support its operation may automatically process limited technical information, including device model and type, operating system and app version, language and device configuration, application usage, crash and diagnostic information, performance data, device or installation identifiers, and Advertising ID where available and processed by applicable platform services.

Such identifiers may be associated with a device or application installation and are not intended to directly identify you by name.

3. How We Use Information

Limited technical information may be processed to operate and maintain the game, understand general application usage, identify technical problems, improve stability and compatibility, improve gameplay and user experience, protect against misuse, and comply with legal and platform requirements.

4. Platform and Third-Party Services

Kivaryn may interact with platform-level services required for application distribution, installation, compatibility, security, and other technical functionality, including Google Play services and Android platform services.

These providers may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Game Progress and Local Storage

Kivaryn may store gameplay information locally on your device, including completed Currents and Route progress, scores and best results, collected Kera, Flow and gameplay statistics, achievements and medals, unlocked and selected Vessel Lab items, and game preferences and settings.

The game does not require an external player account or cloud-based profile for this locally stored progress. Local data may be deleted by resetting progress, clearing application data, or uninstalling the application.

6. In-Game Currency and Purchases

Kivaryn includes an in-game resource called Kera. Kera is earned through gameplay and may be used for certain in-game unlocks and customization options.

Kera has no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real money. The current version of Kivaryn does not offer in-app purchases or real-money transactions.

7. Advertising

The current version of Kivaryn does not display third-party advertising and does not require players to view advertisements to progress or receive gameplay rewards.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Limited technical information may be processed or shared with platform or technical service providers only when necessary to operate and distribute the application, maintain stability and compatibility, investigate technical or security issues, or comply with legal obligations.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until it is deleted, reset, cleared, or removed through application uninstallation.

Technical information processed independently by platform providers may be retained according to those providers' policies, settings, and applicable legal requirements.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with the application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Kivaryn is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. Where applicable, the game is operated in accordance with Google Play policies concerning children and families.

12. User Choices and Controls

Depending on your device and region, you may control certain application or platform data through Android privacy and application settings, device-level privacy controls, clearing locally stored data, using the in-game reset option, or uninstalling the application.

13. User Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you may have rights regarding personal data processed about you, including access, correction, deletion or restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent where applicable, and the right to lodge a complaint with an appropriate authority.

Some technical information may be processed independently by platform providers, and requests concerning that information may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

14. International Data Processing

Platform or technical service providers may process technical information on servers outside your country of residence. Where required, such processing is subject to appropriate safeguards under applicable data protection laws.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game, its functionality, legal requirements, or services used by the application change. Any updated version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

16. Contact Information

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, please contact Marks Dec using the contact information associated with the developer's official distribution channels.
