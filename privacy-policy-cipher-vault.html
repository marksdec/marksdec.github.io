Privacy Policy — Cipher Vault: Codebreaker

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Cipher Vault: Codebreaker, developed and published by Marks Dec.

It explains what information may be collected or processed when you use the game, how that information is used, and what choices may be available to you.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Your name, home address, or identification documents
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, call history, or text messages
  • Your photographs, videos, or other personal files
  • Your financial or payment information
  • Your passwords or private communications

The game does not require you to create a personal user account or provide personal profile information.

2. Technical Information That May Be Collected

The game and services used to support it may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • Device model and device type
  • Operating system and application version
  • Language and general device configuration
  • Application usage and interaction data
  • Anonymous crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Performance and stability information
  • Device identifiers and other technical identifiers
  • Firebase Installation ID or similar application installation identifiers
  • Advertising ID, where available on the device

These identifiers are associated with a particular device or application installation and are not intended to directly identify you by name.

3. How We Use Information

Limited technical information may be used for application analytics, diagnosing crashes and errors, improving stability and compatibility, evaluating gameplay features, preventing misuse, and complying with applicable legal and Google Play requirements.

Advertising ID and other device-related identifiers may be processed for analytics and application measurement purposes.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

The game may use third-party services that provide analytics, diagnostics, application distribution, platform functionality, or similar technical services.

Such services may include Google Play services, Android platform services, and Firebase services such as analytics or crash reporting. These providers may process limited technical data in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Game Progress and Local Storage

Adventure progress, completed daily puzzles, achievements, selected investigator, settings, notes, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

The game does not require an external player account or cloud-based profile unless such functionality is clearly introduced and disclosed in a future version.

Locally stored progress may be deleted if you reset the game, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

6. Advertising

The current version of the game may process the Advertising ID for analytics and application measurement purposes.

If advertising features are added or enabled, applicable providers may process device information, Advertising ID, interaction data, and other technical information in accordance with consent requirements, Google Play policies, and their own privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Limited technical information may be shared with service providers when necessary to provide analytics and diagnostics, maintain and improve the application, investigate errors or security issues, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is deleted, reset, or removed through application uninstallation.

Technical data processed by third-party service providers may be retained for periods determined by those providers, their policies, applicable legal requirements, and retention settings.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with the application from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Cipher Vault: Codebreaker is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. Where applicable, the game is operated in accordance with Google Play policies concerning children and families.

11. User Choices and Controls

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to control certain data processing through Android privacy and permission settings, Advertising ID settings, consent options, clearing locally stored application data, or uninstalling the application.

12. User Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you may have rights to request information, access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where applicable, and lodge a complaint with an appropriate authority.

Some technical data may be held only by third-party service providers, in which case requests may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

13. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process technical information on servers outside your country of residence. Where required, such processing is carried out using appropriate safeguards under applicable data protection laws.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The updated version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

15. Contact Information

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, please contact Marks Dec using the contact information associated with the developer's official distribution channels.
