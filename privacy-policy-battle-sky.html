Privacy Policy — Battle in the Sky

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Battle in the Sky, developed and published by Marks Dec, handles user information.

We respect your privacy and have designed the game to operate without requiring users to provide personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Battle in the Sky does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information.

The game does not request or collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home or physical address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media
  • Financial or payment information
  • Authentication credentials

No account or registration is required to play the game.

2. Game Data and Progress

Game progress, settings, scores, achievements, and other gameplay-related information are stored locally on the user's device.

This information is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Marks Dec.

Users can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's data through their device settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Analytics and Tracking

Battle in the Sky does not use analytics or tracking technologies to monitor users.

We do not use user data for profiling, behavioral tracking, or targeted advertising.

4. Advertising

The game does not display third-party advertisements and does not share user information with advertising networks.

5. In-App Purchases

Battle in the Sky does not contain in-app purchases.

The game does not directly collect or process payment information.

6. Third-Party Services

The game does not use third-party services that collect personal information on behalf of the developer.

If third-party functionality that affects the processing of user data is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information with third parties because the game does not collect such information.

8. Data Retention

Marks Dec does not maintain a server-side database containing personal information or gameplay data from Battle in the Sky.

Locally stored game data remains on the user's device until it is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Battle in the Sky does not require users to submit personal information, Marks Dec does not intentionally collect personal data from children or other users.

If you believe that personal information has nevertheless been provided to us, please contact us through the contact information associated with the developer's official distribution channels.

10. User Rights

Because we do not maintain personal user data on our servers, there is generally no personal information for us to access, modify, transfer, or delete.

Users remain in control of locally stored game information through their device and application settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of Battle in the Sky changes or if changes are required to reflect applicable privacy requirements.

The latest version will be available at the published Privacy Policy URL.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Battle in the Sky, please contact Marks Dec using the contact information associated with the developer's official distribution channels.
